# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**Last updated: 2026-08-12**

## English

Open Island ("the App") is a local-first companion app for AI coding agents. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

### Data Collection

The App does **not** send analytics, telemetry, task data, or agent-session data to a server operated by this project.

### How It Works

- Agent integrations use local files, local processes, app automation, and local inter-process communication on your Mac.
- Task titles and agent-session data stay on your Mac unless you choose to share them through another app or service.
- No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting services are used.
- No third-party SDKs or tracking frameworks are included.

### Local Storage

The App stores preferences in on-device UserDefaults. To-do titles, completion state, and timestamps are stored at `~/Library/Application Support/OpenIsland/tasks.json`. The App also reads and caches local agent-session information needed to render the island. These records are not transmitted to a project-operated server.

### Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at:
https://github.com/VihaanMotwani/open-island-tabs/issues

## 中文

Open Island（“本应用”）是一款本地优先的 AI 编程助手配套应用。我们致力于保护您的隐私。

### 数据收集

本应用**不会**向本项目运营的服务器发送分析数据、遥测数据、任务数据或代理会话数据。

### 工作原理

- 代理集成通过 Mac 上的本地文件、本地进程、App 自动化和本地进程间通信工作。
- 任务标题和代理会话数据保留在您的 Mac 上，除非您主动通过其他 App 或服务分享。
- 不使用任何分析、遥测或崩溃报告服务。
- 不包含任何第三方 SDK 或追踪框架。

### 本地存储

本应用在设备的 UserDefaults 中存储偏好设置。待办标题、完成状态和时间戳存储在 `~/Library/Application Support/OpenIsland/tasks.json`。本应用还会读取和缓存渲染 Island 所需的本地代理会话信息。这些记录不会传输到本项目运营的服务器。

### 联系方式

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请在以下地址提交 issue：
https://github.com/VihaanMotwani/open-island-tabs/issues
