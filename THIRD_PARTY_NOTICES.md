## Boring Notch calendar and motion

The Calendar tab's date strip, agenda layout, and EventKit adapter are adapted
from [TheBoredTeam/boring.notch](https://github.com/TheBoredTeam/boring.notch),
commit `99900bf630a3d3e97fae079df2175993318d51f7`, under the
[GNU General Public License version 3](LICENSE).

Credit to Harsh Vardhan Goswami, Alexander, and the Boring Notch contributors.
The EventKit code also attributes its original Calendr source to Paker.
Open Island adaptations dated September 13, 2026 include a standalone tab,
actor-isolated read-only calendar queries, explicit permission handling,
date-selection race protection, accessibility, and Reduce Motion support.
Opening/closing spring choices were informed by Boring Notch's `ContentView.swift`.

Source paths and integration details are in [docs/calendar.md](docs/calendar.md).
